# Interpretation (100-150 words)

The 40 most abundant taxa were compared across IBS (n=72) and UC (n=58) samples before and after treatment (130 labelled samples; two unlabelled samples were excluded). Groups overlap almost completely in the Bray-Curtis PCoA (PCoA1 27.6%, PCoA2 19.3%). PERMANOVA gives a small effect, R2 = 0.034, F = 1.47, p = 0.088, and the dispersion diagnostic is not significant (betadisper p = 0.618), so the weak separation is not explained by unequal within-group spread. Alpha diversity is flat across groups (Kruskal-Wallis p = 0.279; Shannon mean 1.49). In the differential test (Wilcoxon, UC_before 29 vs IBS_before 36) no taxon reaches FDR < 0.05; the smallest raw p is 0.018 (s__torques, higher in IBS_before), which is expected noise at 40 tests. Limitation: this is a simulated, compositional table with no extraction or sequencing batch covariate, so no causal or diagnostic reading is defensible.
